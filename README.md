**Summary** 

This group project was completed at WBS Coding School and focuses on business analysis using SQL and Tableau. The goal was to evaluate Magist as a potential partner for Eniac by analyzing orders, sellers, product categories, pricing, revenue, and delivery performance.

SQL was used for data extraction and aggregation, while Tableau was used to create visual insights. The analysis shows that Magist is not well suited for high-end technical products due to lower average prices, low tech-seller revenue, and long delivery times. However, the platform still presents an opportunity for controlled expansion into the Brazilian market.

**Languages and libraries used**

SQL, Tableau.

**Key Learnings**

* Practical application of SQL for real-world business analysis, including data filtering, aggregation, joins, and subqueries.
* Translation of raw SQL outputs into clear, decision-oriented visualizations using Tableau.
* Understanding how data insights inform strategic decisions, such as risk assessment and partnership duration.
* Experience working in a team environment on an end-to-end data analytics project, from data extraction to final business recommendations.
* Improved data storytelling skills by connecting analytical results to concrete business conclusions.

**Challenges overcome**

* Understanding and navigating a complex relational database schema with multiple interconnected tables.
* Writing efficient SQL queries to correctly join large datasets.
* Defining meaningful business metrics (e.g. average price, revenue, delivery performance) from raw transactional data.
* Coordinating work within a group setting, including task division, consistency of assumptions, and integration of results into a coherent final narrative.
